# Methods (with the drug-treatment data)

A version without the drug-treatment data is in [METHODS_no_drug.md](METHODS_no_drug.md).

**Replication timing of histone genes.** We used all 108 wavelet-smoothed replication-timing (RT) signal tracks (hg19) released by ENCODE (ENCODE Project Consortium 2020): Repli-chip (86 tracks; Ryba et al. 2010), six-fraction Repli-seq (16; Hansen et al. 2010) and early/late Repli-seq (6; Marchal et al. 2018), from 50 cell lines grouped as cancer (11), non-cancer (29) and stem cells (10). Drug-treatment Repli-seq of JEFF lymphoblastoid cells, untreated or treated with an ATR inhibitor, aphidicolin or both (one sample each), came from Koundrioukoff et al. (2026); for each condition we combined the six cell-cycle fractions (1 kb bins) as a weighted average and smoothed it with a level-7 Daubechies-4 wavelet transform, as for the ENCODE six-fraction tracks (Hansen et al. 2010), and this procedure reproduced the 16 released ENCODE six-fraction tracks from their fraction signals (Pearson r = 0.996-0.998 over 10 kb bins). Genes were the 20,176 protein-coding genes of GENCODE v44 lifted to GRCh37 (Frankish et al. 2021), and the 66 replication-dependent histone genes were those of the HIST1-HIST4 clusters. In every track, the signal of a gene was the mean over its gene body; where a gene contained no data point, we interpolated the signal linearly at the gene midpoint between the two flanking data points, if these were at most 50 kb apart; this applied to about 52% of the histone-gene values in the ENCODE tracks (8% of all gene values) and to 15 of 264 histone-gene values in the drug tracks, all at duplicated HIST2 genes. The RT score of a gene was 1 minus the mid-rank percentile of its signal among the mean signals of consecutive 10 kb bins over chromosomes 1-22 and X of the same track (0, earliest; 1, latest); 11 early housekeeping and 6 late reference genes confirmed the signal orientation of every track. Highly expressed genes were the top 5% of protein-coding genes by mean TPM in ENCODE RNA-seq (RSEM) of each of the 24 cell lines with such data. For each cell line and gene set, we report the median RT score over all tracks of that cell line and all genes of the set; for the drug data, we report per-gene RT scores and their change from the untreated sample. Because the ENCODE cell-line groups are confounded with assay, group comparisons are descriptive. Accessions, checksums and URLs of all inputs are listed in `data/*.csv`. The analysis is a Nextflow pipeline (Di Tommaso et al. 2017) with Python scripts (numpy, pandas, pyBigWig, PyWavelets, matplotlib, seaborn; versions in `uv.lock`).
